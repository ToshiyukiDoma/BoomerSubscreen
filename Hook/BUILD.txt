Compile ojiisansubscreen.c and cJSON.c as a Windows DLL.
Zig 0.13.0 commands:
zig cc -target x86-windows-gnu -shared -Os -s ojiisansubscreen.c cJSON.c -o ojiisansubscreen.dll
zig cc -target x86_64-windows-gnu -shared -Os -s ojiisansubscreen.c cJSON.c -o ojiisansubscreen.dll
Keep the outputs in separate architecture folders.
